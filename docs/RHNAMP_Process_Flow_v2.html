👤 Individual applicants
✔Nigerian citizen or registered agribusiness
✔Youth (18–35) or Women agripreneurs, or Agro-service companies
✔Min. 350 hectares contiguous farmland per tractor
✔Valid business plan: coverage, pricing, revenue projections
✔10–25% equity down payment required
✔Agree to GPS tracking + certified operator only
🏢 Enterprise / company applicants
✔Registered business (CAC cert) or cooperative
✔Min. 500 hectares within 50km radius per tractor
✔Trained tractor operators + booking agents employed
✔TIN + bank statement + operational base in agric zone
✔Commit to operator & technician training participation
✔Digital tracking system adoption mandatory
🚜 What beneficiaries receive
✔Fully equipped tractor + implements (lease-to-own)
✔Free 3-year servicing at BOA-approved hubs
✔GPS IoT tracking device fitted to tractor
✔Business support: bookings, revenue, farmer relations
✔Mandatory training: operations, maintenance, finance
✔⚠ Repossession after 3 consecutive missed payments

Phase 4
Delegated Approval Authority (DAA) — Single-Committee Routing
Steps 9–12 · One committee only, based on applicant type & facility size
⚡
How DAA routing works
Each application is routed to one approving authority only — determined at Step 9 by applicant type and total facility value. The file goes directly to that authority and stops there. It does not pass through lower levels first. This eliminates unnecessary delays for small-ticket individual leases while preserving full governance for large fleet applications.
Approval routing matrix
TIER A
Branch Approval
Who: Youth Agripreneurs & Women Agripreneurs
Tractors: 1–2 tractors per applicant
Approver: Branch Manager
Tenure: 3 years (PAYS)
✔ Decision at branch — no escalation needed
TIER B
Zonal Approval
Who: Youth / Women requesting 3+ tractors OR small agro-service companies
Tractors: 3–9 tractors
Approver: Zonal Manager + Zonal Risk Officer
Tenure: 3–5 years
✔ Decision at zone — branch endorses only
TIER C
HO Approval
Who: Large agro-service companies / fleet operators
Tractors: 10+ tractors
Approver: MD/CEO + ED Credit + Head of Risk
Tenure: Up to 5 years
✔ Head Office decision — highest exposure
⚠
Override rule: If any Tier A or Tier B applicant presents risk indicators flagged during appraisal (e.g. adverse credit history, unusually large land claim, prior BOA default), the Credit Officer may manually escalate to the next tier regardless of facility size. This override must be documented in the credit memo.
