RH-SHF Credit Workflow — End-to-End Guide

Every stage of a Renewed Hope Smallholder Farmer (dry-season input financing) case, from the FAC's profiling submission through to disbursement — who acts, what they do, and the action that moves it to the next stage.

FAC Profiling→ Appraisal→ Risk Review→ Committee→ Ratification→ Offer Acceptance→ Legal Clearance→ Pre-Deployment→ Disbursement→ Approved / Active
Two things to know before you start. (1) The external status is a long plateau: it flips to Under Review the moment the FAC submits profiling and stays there through the entire internal pipeline — what actually moves is the internal stage shown on each card. (2) Two stages are FAC-side (orange) and happen on token-protected pages, not with a staff login: Profiling and Offer Acceptance.

The stages, in order

0

FAC Profiling

status: ProfilingPending → ProfilingInProgress
🌾 FAC (applicant) · token link: /rhshf/profiling/{ref}?token=…
What they do
Open the profiling link and work through the five steps, confirming/entering data and attaching the required documents:
  1. Company Verification — confirm CAC / company details.
  2. Credit Bureau Check — consent / company lookup.
  3. EOP Review — confirm the Economics-of-Production input lines.
  4. Supporting Documents — upload the mandatory items (CAC, off-take, farmer register, warehousing, audited financials — a 3-year template is offered here to download, fill, and upload).
  5. Review & Submit — final review, then submit.
Advances when
The FAC submits the final step. External status flips to Under Review and the internal stage becomes Appraisal — the case now appears in the Credit Officer's RH-SHF queue.
1

Appraisal

stage: AppraisalUnder Review
👤 Credit Officer · uche.eze@crms.ng
What they do
Open the case and build the credit picture: run Re-run Credit Checks (bureau for the company + every director/guarantor with a BVN); capture Directors & Guarantors as needed; complete the Financial Appraisal (crop-economics viability — DSCR, margins, IRR, break-even); optionally capture the Financial Analysis (3-year statements). Review Documents, Collateral and the AI Advisory.
Advances when
Credit Officer clicks Proceed → Risk Review.
Other outcomes
Return to FAC (reopens profiling) · Decline (terminal).
2

Risk Review

stage: RiskReviewUnder Review
👤 Risk Manager · chukwuemeka.obi@crms.ng
What they do
Independently review the appraisal, bureau results and advisory for risk concerns.
Advances when
Risk Manager clicks Clear → Committee Voting.
Other outcomes
Return to FAC · Decline (terminal).
3

Committee Voting

stage: CommitteeVotingUnder Review
👥 Committee Members · tier depends on EOP value (Branch / Zonal / Regional / Head Office)
What they do
Each member of the routed committee opens the case and casts a vote: Approve, Reject, Abstain, or Return to FAC. The Committee tab shows the required number of votes and minimum approvals for that tier. Members must be distinct people (maker-checker).
Advances when
The required Approve threshold is reached — the case then advances to Ratification automatically on the deciding vote.
Other outcomes
Tally reaches Reject → Declined (terminal) · committee Return to FAC.
4

Ratification

stage: Ratification → OfferGeneratedUnder Review
👤 Final Approver · yusuf.mohammed@crms.ng
What they do
Final sign-off. Must be a different person from the appraiser, risk officer and approving committee members.
Advances when
Final Approver clicks Ratify with the approved amount equal to the Total EOP value exactly (no partial approval). This generates the offer letter PDF and moves the case to Awaiting Offer Acceptance.
Other outcomes
Return to FAC · Decline (terminal).
5

Offer Acceptance

stage: AwaitingOfferAcceptance
🌾 FAC (applicant) · token link: /rhshf/offer/{ref}?token=…
What they do
On the token-protected offer page, the FAC downloads the offer, uploads the signed copy, and accepts. This is not a staff action — the CRMS Offer tab is view-only.
Advances when
The FAC accepts the offer → Legal Clearance.
Other outcomes
FAC rejects the offer → Cancelled (a withdrawal, not a bank decision).
6

Legal Clearance

stage: LegalClearanceUnder Review
👤 Legal Officer · adaeze.nwosu@crms.ng
What they do
Review the legal/security position; optionally Record Collateral (bank guarantee / NIRSAL CRG / legal mortgage).
Advances when
Legal Officer clicks Grant → Pre-Deployment Verification.
Other outcomes
Return to Ratification · Decline (terminal).
7

Pre-Deployment Verification

stage: PreDeploymentVerificationUnder Review
👤 Disbursement Officer · folake.balogun@crms.ng
What they do
Open the Pre-Deployment Gate and confirm each checklist item (final conditions before funds move).
Advances when
All checklist items are confirmed → the gate completes → Disbursement.
8

Disbursement

stage: Disbursement → ActiveUnder Review → Approved
👤 Disbursement Officer · folake.balogun@crms.ng
What they do
Click Book Disbursement, enter the input-supplier account, and book the loan in the core banking / Fineract system. The disbursed amount must equal the ratified amount.
Advances when
Booking succeeds → external status flips to Approved (the credit decision, permanent) and the internal stage becomes Active.
9

Active → Closed

stage: Active → ClosedApproved
👤 Disbursement Officer / Operations · folake.balogun@crms.ng
What they do
Monitor the live facility on the Loan Account tab through the season.
Advances when
When the facility is settled, click Mark as Closed → Closed. End of lifecycle.

Rules that will block you if missed

  • Maker-checker (distinct people). The ratifier must differ from the appraiser, risk officer and approving committee members; the committee needs a quorum of distinct members. Don't use one admin account for the decision stages.
  • Exact amounts. The ratification amount must equal the Total EOP value exactly, and the disbursed amount must equal the ratified amount — no rounding, no partial approval.
  • The two FAC steps aren't staff actions. Profiling (step 0) and Offer Acceptance (step 5) happen on token-protected pages; there's no staff button for them. Use the FAC links.
  • External status is a plateau. From FAC submission to disbursement the external status reads Under Review — track progress by the internal stage, not the status.
