RH-SHF Credit Workflow — End-to-End Guide

Every stage of a Renewed Hope Smallholder Farmer (dry-season input financing) case, from the FAC's profiling submission through to disbursement — who acts, what they do, and the action that moves it to the next stage.

FAC Profiling→ Appraisal→ Legal Clearance→ Risk Review→ Committee→ Ratification→ Offer Acceptance→ Pre-Deployment→ Disbursement→ Approved / Active
Three things to know before you start. (1) The external status is a long plateau: it flips to Under Review the moment the FAC submits profiling and stays there through the entire internal pipeline — what actually moves is the internal stage shown on each card. (2) Two stages are FAC-side (orange) and happen on token-protected pages, not with a staff login: Profiling and Offer Acceptance. (3) Legal Clearance now runs second, straight after appraisal and before the committee votes — so the security is sighted and perfected before anyone approves the facility, and an approved case can no longer be killed by a legal finding afterwards.
What changed in October 2026. Legal Clearance moved from after offer acceptance to between Appraisal and Risk Review. Risk is now the last internal review before the committee, with the legal opinion in front of it. Three consequences: the FAC must declare at least one security instrument with its document attached before it can submit; only the Legal Officer can mark an instrument perfected, and only with the evidence on file; and if the committee approves a case whose security is not perfected, it must say whether perfection is deferred (a blocking pre-deployment condition) or waived.

The stages, in order

0

FAC Profiling

status: ProfilingPending → ProfilingInProgress
🌾 FAC (applicant) · token link: /rhshf/profiling/{ref}?token=…
What they do
Open the profiling link and work through the five steps, confirming/entering data and attaching the required documents. The FAC originates its own parties and security here — officers later verify, they don't scout for it:
  1. Company Verification — confirm CAC / company details.
  2. Credit Bureau Check & Parties — company lookup, plus the FAC declares the subjects the bureau checks run on:
    • Directors — list every director; a BVN is required for each (pull from the Bank of Agriculture record, or add by hand). Missing BVNs can be filled inline.
    • Guarantors — individuals (checked by BVN, required) or companies (checked by RC number).
    • Collateral / Security — one or more instruments (bank guarantee / NIRSAL CRG / legal mortgage); the type-specific fields show for the chosen type, and a supporting document is attached to each instrument.
  3. EOP Review & Farm Plan — confirm the Economics-of-Production input lines and enter the farm plan (at least one crop is required).
  4. Supporting Documents — upload the mandatory items (CAC, off-take, Farmers Register, warehousing, audited financials — a 3-year template is offered here to download, fill, and upload — and the Credit Appraisal Memorandum (CAM), which is optional on the portal at Expression of Interest but compulsory before the EOP reaches credit appraisal). Where the FAC supplied a CAM on an earlier season, it can be re-used from the slot instead of uploaded again; a copy is attached to this application and both the FAC and the reviewing officer see which case it came from and when it was first supplied.
  5. Review & Submit — a full read-back of every section, each with an Edit link that jumps back to fix it and returns here. Submit is blocked until: every director and every individual guarantor has a BVN; the farm plan and mandatory documents are satisfied; and at least one security instrument is declared with a document attached to every instrument.
The FAC can move back and forward between any step already reached (click a completed step, or an Edit link on the review) to correct a record — there is no need to wait for a staff Return-to-FAC for self-corrections. Every uploaded file can be previewed in a modal or downloaded without leaving the form, so the FAC can check it attached the right document.
Advances when
The FAC submits the final step. External status flips to Under Review and the internal stage becomes Appraisal — the case now appears in the Credit Officer's RH-SHF queue.
1

Appraisal

stage: AppraisalUnder Review
👤 Credit Officer · uche.eze@crms.ng
What they do
Open the case and build the credit picture: run Re-run Credit Checks (bureau for the company + every director/guarantor BVN the FAC supplied at profiling); verify the FAC-declared Directors, Guarantors and Collateral (the Credit Officer confirms, not originates); complete the Financial Appraisal (crop-economics viability — DSCR, margins, IRR, break-even); optionally capture the Financial Analysis (3-year statements). Review Documents, Collateral and the AI Advisory.
Advances when
Credit Officer clicks Proceed → Legal Clearance. A financial appraisal must be saved first.
Other outcomes
Return to FAC (reopens profiling) · Decline (terminal).
2

Legal Clearance

stage: LegalClearanceUnder Review
👤 Legal Officer · adaeze.nwosu@crms.ng
What they do
Confirm the security the FAC declared at profiling — instrument by instrument, on the Collateral tab. The tab shows how many of how many are confirmed; each row has a Confirm button that opens the instrument with its attached evidence. For each one: check it against its document, add any title reference / registration authority established during perfection, write a note on that instrument, attach further evidence if needed, then Mark as perfected.

This is a confirmation step, not data entry: nothing is re-keyed, and staff cannot add an instrument here. The FAC's own figures and note are left exactly as submitted — the legal note is stored separately. An instrument the FAC never declared is a Return to FAC.

Perfected cannot be set without a document on the instrument — no evidence, no assertion. Only the Legal Officer can set it; nobody can set it at declaration time.
Advances when
Grant → Risk Review. Grant is only available once every instrument is perfected.
Other outcomes
Grant with Caveat → Risk Review with the defect documented, for security that could not be perfected — the committee then has to answer for it · Return to FAC at a chosen profiling step, for a fixable defect · Decline (terminal) for something fundamental, such as falsified title.
3

Risk Review

stage: RiskReviewUnder Review
👤 Risk Manager · chukwuemeka.obi@crms.ng
What they do
The last internal review before the case is circulated. Independently review the appraisal, bureau results and advisory for risk concerns — now with the legal opinion already on file, including any caveat about security that could not be perfected.
Advances when
Risk Manager clicks Clear → Committee Voting. Must be a different person from the appraiser and from the Legal Officer who cleared the case.
Other outcomes
Return to FAC · Decline (terminal).
4

Committee Voting

stage: CommitteeVotingUnder Review
👥 Committee Members · tier depends on EOP value (Branch / Zonal / Regional / Head Office)
What they do
Each member of the routed committee opens the case and casts a vote: Approve, Reject, Abstain, or Return to FAC. The Committee tab shows the required number of votes and minimum approvals for that tier. Members must be distinct people, and distinct from the appraiser, the Legal Officer and the risk officer (maker-checker).

If the security is not fully perfected — because Legal granted with a caveat — an approving vote must say on what basis, and the modal shows Legal's caveat alongside the choice:
  • Perfect before disbursement (deferred) — the approval stands, and perfection becomes a mandatory pre-deployment condition. No money moves until it is done.
  • Waive perfection — the committee accepts the facility without that security, recorded deliberately against the decision.
If members disagree, the stricter reading wins: one member insisting on perfection binds the case. The recorded disposition is badged on the Committee tab and carried into the ratification screen.
Advances when
The required Approve threshold is reached — the case then advances to Ratification automatically on the deciding vote.
Other outcomes
Tally reaches Reject → Declined (terminal) · committee Return to FAC.
5

Ratification

stage: Ratification → OfferGeneratedUnder Review
👤 Final Approver · yusuf.mohammed@crms.ng
What they do
Final sign-off. Must be a different person from the appraiser, the Legal Officer, the risk officer and the approving committee members.

The Ratify modal carries the facility in front of the ratifier: borrower and committee tier; interest rate, crop cycle and amount repayable at harvest; the appraisal in five figures (DSCR, gross margin, IRR, yield and price headroom, each coloured by pass/fail) with gates passed, repayment capacity and the credit officer's recommendation; the legal outcome and its caveat; the committee tally and its perfection disposition; and the security — instrument count, how many are perfected, value covered.
Advances when
Final Approver clicks Ratify with the approved amount equal to the Total EOP value exactly (no partial approval). This generates the offer letter PDF and moves the case to Awaiting Offer Acceptance.
Other outcomes
Return to FAC · Decline (terminal).
6

Offer Acceptance

stage: AwaitingOfferAcceptance
🌾 FAC (applicant) · token link: /rhshf/offer/{ref}?token=…
What they do
On the token-protected offer page, the FAC downloads the offer, uploads the signed copy, and accepts. This is not a staff action — the CRMS Offer tab is view-only.
Advances when
The FAC accepts the offer → Pre-Deployment Verification. There is no legal stage after acceptance any more; the pre-deployment gate seeds its checklist at this point.
Other outcomes
FAC rejects the offer → Cancelled (a withdrawal, not a bank decision).
7

Pre-Deployment Verification

stage: PreDeploymentVerificationUnder Review
👤 Disbursement Officer · folake.balogun@crms.ng
What they do
Open the Pre-Deployment Gate and confirm each checklist item (final conditions before funds move) — BOA account verified against core banking, collateral reviewed, signed offer documents, compliance sign-off.

If the committee approved with perfection deferred, the gate also carries a mandatory item — “Collateral Perfection Completed (committee condition)” — added automatically at offer acceptance. The gate cannot complete while it is unconfirmed, so a deferral genuinely blocks the money rather than being a note nobody chases.
Advances when
All mandatory checklist items are confirmed → the gate completes → Disbursement.
8

Disbursement

stage: Disbursement → ActiveUnder Review → Approved
👤 Disbursement Officer · folake.balogun@crms.ng
What they do
Click Book Disbursement, enter the input-supplier account, and book the loan in the core banking / Fineract system. The disbursed amount must equal the ratified amount.
Advances when
Booking succeeds → external status flips to Approved (the credit decision, permanent) and the internal stage becomes Active.

Active → Closed

Rules that will block you if missed

  • Maker-checker (distinct people). Four distinct people are now needed before ratification: the Credit Officer, the Legal Officer, the Risk Manager and the committee must all differ, and the ratifier must differ from all of them. Don't use one admin account for the decision stages — the stage will refuse the second action.
  • Security must exist before a case can be submitted. At least one instrument, and a document attached to every instrument. The FAC cannot submit otherwise.
  • Only Legal marks an instrument perfected, and only with evidence on file. It cannot be set when the instrument is declared, and it cannot be changed after the case leaves legal clearance — the committee voted on what was asserted.
  • Plain Grant means fully perfected. If any instrument is unperfected, Grant is unavailable; use Grant with Caveat, and the committee then records whether perfection is deferred or waived.
  • Exact amounts. The ratification amount must equal the Total EOP value exactly, and the disbursed amount must equal the ratified amount — no rounding, no partial approval.
  • The two FAC steps aren't staff actions. Profiling (step 0) and Offer Acceptance (step 6) happen on token-protected pages; there's no staff button for them. Use the FAC links. Profiling links are single-use — the first page load consumes the token, after which the session carries the FAC; a fresh link is minted on request.
  • External status is a plateau. From FAC submission to disbursement the external status reads Under Review — track progress by the internal stage, not the status.
  • BVNs are required to submit profiling. Every director, and every individual guarantor, must have a BVN before the FAC can submit (companies are checked by RC instead). The farm plan and all mandatory documents must also be satisfied.
  • FAC originates parties & security. Directors, guarantors and collateral (with evidence) are entered by the FAC during profiling — officers verify and perfect them, they don't scout for them later, and staff cannot add an instrument at all. One the FAC never declared is a Return to FAC.
  • Everyone can see every case; only the stage's owner can act. The RH-SHF queue shows all stages to every actor, so a case is findable whatever stage it is in. Acting is still gated by stage and role, and a stage already acted on for that cycle will refuse a second action — unless the case was returned to the FAC and resubmitted, which opens a new cycle and reopens every stage.
