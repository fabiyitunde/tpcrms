Origination & Branch Review

Stages 1–4: Loan Officer (Maker) prepares; Branch Approver (Checker) reviews

Maker — Stages 1–3
Loan Officer (Branch scope)
Creates application; captures borrower details and loan purpose
Registers Directors, Signatories, Beneficial Owners (25%+ stake)
Uploads all required documents (CAC, Board Resolution, Tax Clearance, IDs, etc.)
Uploads and confirms bank statements (manual or CBS pull)
Uploads financial statements (Audited, Management Accounts, Projections)
Registers guarantors with KYC and proposes collateral assets
Submits complete package for Branch Review
→
Checker — Stage 4
Branch Approver (Branch scope)
Reviews completeness and quality of the submitted package
System blocks approval if Approval Gate is not satisfied
Must provide a comment when making any decision

Outcomes:
✅ Approve → advances to Credit Analysis
↩ Return → back to Loan Officer for correction
✕ Reject → application terminated
⛔Approval Gate 1 — Branch Review (all required before approval)
  • All documents uploaded and verified
  • Bank statements uploaded and confirmed
  • Financial statements uploaded and confirmed

Credit Analysis & HO Review

Stages 5–6: Credit Officer (Maker, 48 hrs) → HO Reviewer (Checker, 24 hrs)

Maker — Credit Analysis
Credit Officer
Reviews financial statements — ratios, trends, viability
Runs bank statement cashflow analysis (AI-categorised transactions)
Orders credit bureau checks via SmartComply (4 providers)
Triggers AI Advisory risk scoring across 8 risk categories
Reviews and approves/rejects each guarantor
Initiates collateral valuation; confirms coverage adequacy
Prepares credit memo with recommendation
Refers completed package to HO Reviewer
→
Checker — HO Review
HO Reviewer
Reviews the complete credit analysis package
Reviews bureau outputs, AI advisory, and credit memo
Reviews legal opinion (Legal Officer + Head of Legal sign-off)
System checks Approval Gate 2 before allowing circulation
Approves for committee and assembles digital Loan Pack

Outcomes:
✅ Approve → Loan Pack circulated to Committee
↩ Return → back to Credit Officer for rework
⛔Approval Gate 2 — HO Review (all required before committee circulation)
  • All documents verified
  • Bank statements verified
  • Financial statements confirmed
  • All collateral assets valued
  • All guarantors approved
  • Cashflow analysis completed

Legal Track — Parallel Workflow

Legal Officer (Maker) → Head of Legal (Checker) — runs concurrently with Credit Analysis

Maker
Legal Officer
Reviews title instruments and ownership documents for each collateral asset
Prepares a formal legal opinion on each collateral item
Validates encumbrances, lien registration, and existing charges
Drafts security and perfection documentation
Records legal clearance on collateral (status: Approved)
Tracks lien perfection: Lien Registered → Perfected
→
Checker
Head of Legal
Reviews legal opinion prepared by Legal Officer
Countersigns — dual legal sign-off required before committee
Signed legal opinion is included in the committee Loan Pack
Confirms perfection status of all collateral assets
Final legal clearance recorded on the application record

Committee Circulation & Voting

Stages 7–8: HO Reviewer circulates → Committee Members vote — SLA: 72 hours

C
Committee Process
Multiple checkers — each member votes independently
  • 1HO Reviewer selects committee and circulates digital Loan Pack
  • 2Loan Pack auto-assembles: credit memo, financial analysis, bureau reports, AI advisory, legal opinion, collateral summary
  • 3Each Committee Member receives notification and reviews the full pack
  • 4Each member casts a formal vote: Approve / Reject / Abstain
  • 5Members may attach comments (Internal or Committee visibility)
  • 6System aggregates votes and records the committee decision

Approval Gates

System-enforced checkpoints that block workflow advancement until all conditions are satisfied

⛔Gate 1 — Branch Review
  • All required documents uploaded and verified
  • Bank statements uploaded and confirmed
  • Financial statements uploaded and confirmed
⛔Gate 2 — HO Review (for Committee)
  • All documents verified
  • Bank statements verified
  • Financial statements confirmed
  • All collateral assets valued
  • All guarantors approved (credit-check complete)
  • Bank statement cashflow analysis completed
⛔Gate 3 — Final Approval
  • All collateral assets approved — legal clearance recorded AND credit sign-off on each asset
⛔Gate 4 — Disbursement
  • Offer letter countersigned by applicant
  • All Conditions Precedent marked Satisfied
  • Disbursement memo prepared and uploaded
  • GM Finance approval received
Override: Risk Manager may bypass any non-strict gate with a documented override note. All overrides are permanently recorded in the audit trail with the actor, timestamp, and justification.

Integration Architecture

Six external system integrations — CRMS as the central credit hub

Apache Fineract
Core Banking System (CBS)
Loan product sync from CBS; account data lookup; loan schedule calculation; loan account creation at disbursement; repayment schedule retrieval
SmartComply
Credit Bureau Aggregator
Single integration covering CreditRegistry, FirstCentral, CRC, and SmartComply direct. 15+ report types: individual, corporate, fraud, KYC (BVN, NIN, CAC, Passport, Driver's Licence)
AI Advisory Engine
Risk Intelligence Platform
Automated risk scoring across 8 risk categories. Outputs risk rating (Very Low to Very High) and actionable recommendation (Strong Approve to Decline)
Mono Connect
Open Banking / Account Data
Direct bank statement retrieval via open banking APIs — verified, machine-readable transaction data for cashflow analysis; supplements manual uploads
Notification Services
Multi-Channel Communications
Email, SMS, WhatsApp, In-App, and Push notifications. Template-driven with 15+ event triggers. Priority levels: Low / Normal / High / Urgent
Document Generation
PDF & Report Engine
Server-side generation of offer letters, credit memos, loan packs (15 sections), and disbursement memos. Template-managed; version-controlled output
